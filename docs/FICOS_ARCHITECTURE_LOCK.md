# FICOS Architecture Lock

## Locked components

- Canonical leakage-safe expanding walk-forward forecasting.
- Empirical validation-residual uncertainty.
- Selective WHEN decision with explicit abstention.
- Separate HOW contract alternatives.
- Historical market opportunity representation with provenance.
- Deterministic MILP and constraint stress testing.
- Robust and mean-CVaR layers as research options, not proven value sources.
- Counterfactual policy comparison and claim audit.

## Rejected or prohibited

- Fabricated SAIL procurement records.
- Claims of actual SAIL savings.
- Unnecessary new models, agents, cloud infrastructure, or production systems.
- Treating scenario contract economics as observed facts.

## Remaining limitation

The private-data boundary is unresolved by design. If real SAIL data becomes available, validate it against `data/procurement_ledger_template.csv`, run the validator, and then perform a separate ledger-backed evaluation. Until then, this architecture is locked as a public-data historical market-counterfactual research system.
